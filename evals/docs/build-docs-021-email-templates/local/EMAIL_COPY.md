# Sign-up email

What the confirmation email says. Everything here is final.

- Subject: Welcome to Harbour Studio, confirm your email
- Heading: You are almost in
- Message: Hi (their nickname), one tap and you can book your first class.
- Button: Confirm and book

The button is the only way to confirm. The email has no code.

# Password change email

A short heads-up for the person whose password was changed.

- Subject: Your Harbour Studio password was changed
- Message: If this was not you, reply to this email and we will lock your account.
